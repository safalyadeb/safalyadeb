## About Me

I'm a Computer Science & Technology diploma student with a strong interest in software development and the technology behind the web. My journey started with frontend fundamentals, and I'm now growing into backend development, learning how to design APIs, model data, and connect everything into complete applications.

I care about building things that are practical, readable, and able to scale as they grow. I don't claim to know everything yet; what I do bring is consistency, curiosity, and a habit of learning by building real projects.
